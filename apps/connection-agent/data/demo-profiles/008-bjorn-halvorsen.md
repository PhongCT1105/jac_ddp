Forty-one years on the water between Bergen and the islands, and I can tell from a passenger's shoulders whether they are commuting, grieving, or running toward something. The fjord teaches you what people will not say - a hand gripping the rail too tight usually means something rougher than the crossing. I care more about steadiness than speed, in a boat and in a conversation.

Two winters left before I hand over the wheelhouse, and I am restless about what follows decades of knowing, almost to the minute, what the weather will do to me. I would like to talk with people who traded routine for open water, or anyone curious how you read a swell, or a stranger, before either one turns.
