Some mornings counting the flock feels like the only honest thing I do all day; other mornings I catch myself pricing the hillside in my head, working out what it would fetch if I stopped. Fifty-four, third generation on this same Navarre slope, and lately the arithmetic of wool and lamb prices keeps losing to the arithmetic of my knees. I still read a coming storm by how the flock bunches before the sky admits anything, still know a sick ewe by the angle she holds her head. What I'm short on is someone outside my own head to argue the decision with, ideally someone who walked away from land, or stayed against the numbers, and can say honestly which one they regret.
