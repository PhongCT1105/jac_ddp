Sixty-three years here and I still change my opinion of the well water with the week's humidity. The shop came to me from my teacher, not my father, and the town gossiped about that for a year before deciding it was fine. He taught me flour behaves however the water tells it to, so I spend more mornings than customers guess just tasting the well, adjusting nothing yet, only waiting. No children are lined up to inherit this, and I have stopped pretending that doesn't occupy me on the walk home. I want company for that walk: people who took over something rather than built it, anyone who has argued with water, or a younger cook patient enough for my slow way of explaining things.
