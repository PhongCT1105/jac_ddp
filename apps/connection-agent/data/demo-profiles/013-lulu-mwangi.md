Seven months ago a hairline crack in my tibia took me off the red dirt roads outside Eldoret and onto a stationary bike for what felt endless. I'm running again now, rebuilding mileage slower than my patience prefers, and the pause taught me more about pacing than any fast season did. This year I started coaching a small group of girls from town on weekend mornings, teaching what nobody taught me early: how to read your own body, how to fuel, how rest counts as training. I'd welcome talk with physiotherapists or coaches about sane return-to-running protocols, and anyone building programs for young athletes where facilities are thin.
