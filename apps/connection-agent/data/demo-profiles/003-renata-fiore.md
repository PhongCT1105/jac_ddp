Four in the morning is when the ovens finally sound like mine again, before the twins wake and the day gets claimed by other people's needs. Twelve years I've turned out sfogliatelle and babà from a small counter near the Spanish Quarter, and the last two rebuilding what ambition means on four hours of sleep. I used to chase awards; now a good day is dough that proofed right and staying upright past the afternoon collapse. I still want the bigger project, a school for kids who want hands that know dough, and I'd like company from other parents and small makers relearning how to want things.
