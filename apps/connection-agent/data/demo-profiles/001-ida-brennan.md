Thirty-one years I carried mail along the same six miles of coastline, and somewhere around year eight it stopped feeling like a route and started feeling like a conversation with the tide. I can name a willet or a whimbrel by its call before I spot the bird, and the low tide still empties me out of bed before the truck ever did, restless until I've covered the same stretch of shingle on foot. What I want now is company for that restlessness: people who track small recurring things and want to talk about what the noticing adds up to. I'm teaching myself to sketch the birds I used to only hear, badly so far, and I'd welcome anyone willing to sit on a jetty and compare notes.
