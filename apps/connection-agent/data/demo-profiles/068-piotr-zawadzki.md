---
Sixty-three, still opening every game the way I did at nineteen: too eager, too much hope in the center squares. The title I meant to earn never came; instead came thirty years of children at a Gdańsk community centre, learning to lose without crying by watching me do it first. Bring me your worst blunder — I'll tell you what it was protecting.
