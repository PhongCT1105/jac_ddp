Ice keeps a different calendar than Sofia does. After a season on the Antarctic Peninsula, where a week can pass without a new sound, I come home and find the ordinary world faintly absurd - queuing, small talk about traffic. Two decades drilling cores, reading what trapped air says about climates centuries gone, and what stays with me isn't the data but the silence, and how it rearranges what counts as urgent. I'm 44, and now want to turn those cores into something a non-scientist would read. I'm looking for people who've relearned ordinary life after long absence, and anyone patient enough to argue about deep time over coffee.
