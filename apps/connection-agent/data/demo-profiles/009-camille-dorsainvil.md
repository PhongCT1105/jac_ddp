Four nights a week I stand behind a piano trio or a singer, holding down the low end so someone else's idea can go somewhere. The other three I teach beginners to find one steady note before they chase a scale. Both jobs are really the same question: how do you support without disappearing, and lead without taking over. I moved to Montreal for a conservatory program and stayed for the rooms, the late calls, the way a good rhythm section can read a soloist's next move before they play it. Lately I'm reading about listening as a discipline, not a metaphor. A hospice nurse is who I want across the table, because that job is only reading a room you can't fix and knowing when to speak.
