I spend my days on the logistics of a new life: apartment leases, school enrollments, a bus route explained three times, someone's first Michigan winter coat. I arrived in Detroit at seven through the same agency where I now sit on the other side of the desk, so I know how much a caseworker's tone on day one can shape the next decade. What holds my attention is the gap between policy and the actual Tuesday someone is having; I keep notes on which forms trip people up and why. Outside work I cook for people I love and want conversations with anyone who has rebuilt a life from scratch, in any direction, for any reason.
